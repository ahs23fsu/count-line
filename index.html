How CountLine works

  • Upload any song. Everything runs on your phone — nothing is uploaded to a server.
  • We find the beats across the whole track, pick the count length from the time signature, and build a song map.
  • Press play: the big number shows the current count, and the dots show where you are in the 8.
  • ⚡ Tempo change markers on the map show where the song speeds up or slows down — your team's trouble spots.
  • Use ⟲ 8 / 8 ⟳ to jump back or forward one full count, and slow the playback speed to rehearse.
  • Turn on 🔊 Voice counts to hear the numbers called out over the music — great for rehearsing without watching the screen. Choose every count, just 1, or 1 & 5.
  • Export a printable count sheet to share with the whole line.
